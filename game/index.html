BANDAR NUSA

An open-world crime story — repay the debt, or the city owns you.

You are Raka. You owe Boss Jalak Rp 1,400,000, and he does not wait. Run his five jobs across the city — the pier, the shops, a stolen car, the harbor thugs — and pay every rupiah back. Watch the stars: the police are always watching you.

WASD — move / drive  ·  Shift — run
E — enter or exit a car  ·  F — punch
Drag mouse — look around  ·  M — music on/off  ·  P — save

An original game. No content from any other game series. Music is synthesized live in your browser.
